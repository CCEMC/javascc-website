Feature Previews

Click a module below to inspect its key settings and mechanics.

AutoMace + Stun Slam

Detects when opponents attempt to block with a shield. Automatically swaps to an axe to instantly break their guard before performing a high-velocity mace smash.

Stun Slam: Enabled Swap Delay: 0-20 Ticks Silent Mode: Available

AutoShieldBreaker

Visibly or silently switches to your axe when attacking a blocking target. Features a customizable Swap Back setting and tick-based delay for seamless, natural movement.

Swap Back: Toggle Swap Delay: Adjustable Silent: Network Packet Mode

SafeAnchor

Automatically places protective obsidian or blocks between you and active respawn anchors to prevent lethality, featuring smooth custom rotation limits up to 90°.

Max Rot Speed: 90° Legit Flick Use Obsidian / Glowstone

SnapTap & AutoClicker

SnapTap: SOCD (Simultaneous Opposite Cardinal Direction) input priority for instantly sharper strafing.
AutoClicker: Highly customized clicker located in the Misc category for precise CPS control.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about javascc.

Yes! javascc is 100% free and open to the community.

javascc is built for Modern Minecraft on Fabric loader (1.21+), supporting all modern weapon mechanics like the Mace and Heavy Core combat.

Simply download javascc v1.0.jar, install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.21+, and drop the JAR directly into your .minecraft/mods folder (or your launcher instances directory).

Press the default keybind (Right Shift) in-game to toggle the dark-mode ClickGUI screen.
