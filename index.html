Feature Previews

Explore every single module category and its underlying mechanics.

Sword Combat Suite

AutoShieldBreaker: Automatically detects opponent shield blocking, swaps to axe, and respects Swap Back & Swap Delay timers.
Criticals: Packet-based or legit mini-jump critical hit simulation.
AimAssist, KillAura, Reach, TriggerBot, Hitboxes: Complete tracking and targeting precision.

AutoShieldBreaker Criticals AimAssist KillAura Hitboxes

Crystal & Anchor Dominance

SafeAnchor: Places obsidian/blocks between you and exploding anchors, capped at 90° smooth rotations.
AutoCrystal & AutoHitCrystal: High-speed crystal placement & break cycles.
AnchorMacro, Surround, AutoTotem, HoverTotem, AutoArmor, AutoExp: Unbreakable defense.

SafeAnchor AutoCrystal Surround AutoTotem AutoExp (Look Down)

1.21 Mace Mechanics

AutoMace (Stun Slam): Automatically breaks enemy shields with an axe prior to delivering high-damage mace strikes.
BreachSwap & AutoWindcharge: Enables instant Breach enchantment weapon swaps and automated windcharge launching.
KeyPearl & AutoPearlCatch: Clutch pearl management.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about javascc.

Yes! javascc is 100% free and open to the community.

javascc is built for Modern Minecraft on Fabric loader (1.21+), supporting all modern weapon mechanics like the Mace and Heavy Core combat.

Simply download javascc v1.0.jar, install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.21+, and drop the JAR directly into your .minecraft/mods folder (or your launcher instances directory).

Press the default keybind (Right Shift) in-game to toggle the dark-mode ClickGUI screen.
